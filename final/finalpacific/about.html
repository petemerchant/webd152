About Pacific Trails Resort

After wrapping up their earlier "community building projects," which of course went completely according to plan and definitely did not end in controversy, Jim Jones, Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh, and Sun Myung Moon decided the world was clearly ready for their next big idea. After all, when three well known spiritual entrepreneurs get together, the only reasonable step is to open a scenic coastal retreat.

Their new project, Pacific Trails Resort, sits just off California State Route 1 near the dramatic cliffs and foggy views of Big Sur. Guests are promised a relaxing escape from everyday life, complete with ocean air, quiet reflection, and the occasional life altering revelation about the meaning of existence. According to the founders, the resort is not just a vacation spot, it is a place for true spiritual awakening, personal growth, and perhaps a gentle suggestion that visitors reconsider their attachment to worldly possessions like money, property, and independent thought.

The three leaders insist Pacific Trails is simply a peaceful community where people can reconnect with nature, discover their higher purpose, and absolutely not participate in anything resembling a cult. Any rumors about loyalty pledges, dramatic sermons at sunrise, or oddly coordinated robes are clearly misunderstandings. It is just a friendly coastal retreat run by three completely trustworthy spiritual guides. What could possibly go wrong.
